How repairs work

Whether you have had a minor car-park scrape or a multi-panel collision, you deal directly with the panel beaters assessing and repairing your vehicle at 72 Nicholson Street.

Book an inspection
  1. 01

    Drive-in inspection & written quote

    Bring your car to 72 Nicholson St on weekdays between 9:00 am and 5:00 pm. We inspect the damage in person and walk you through repair vs replacement options.

  2. 02

    Strip-down, panel beating & alignment

    Damaged trims and panels are removed, underlying brackets are checked, and metal or replacement panels are test-fitted to factory shut lines.

  3. 03

    Surface prep, primer & spray booth finish

    Panels are primed, blocked smooth, colour-matched to your vehicle paint code, and finished with durable clear coat.

  4. 04

    Reassembly, polish & handover

    Bumpers, lights and trims are refitted, paintwork is machine-polished, and we call you as soon as your car is ready for collection.
